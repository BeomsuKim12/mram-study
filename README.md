# MRAM Study Portfolio

This repository contains my self-directed study on MRAM (Magnetoresistive Random Access Memory) and spintronics.

## Overview
- Studied MRAM device physics, including MTJ structure and spin transport
- Analyzed key trade-offs such as thermal stability, switching current, scaling, and process integration
- Extended understanding from device level to system-level concepts such as memory hierarchy and in-memory computing

## Contents

### Reports
- Material-centric analysis of MRAM
- Fundamentals of MRAM and memory computing

### Presentation
- Structural limitations of MRAM and future directions

## Motivation
During my exploration of academic and research directions, I became interested in spintronics and MRAM through coursework and discussions.

This led me to independently study the physical principles, structural challenges, and future potential of MRAM, and I found strong interest in this field.

I plan to continue studying device physics, process integration, and system-level applications, and further develop my understanding toward research in semiconductor memory technologies.
