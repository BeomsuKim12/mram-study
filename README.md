# MRAM Study Portfolio

This repository documents my self-directed study on MRAM (Magnetoresistive Random Access Memory) and spintronics, focusing on device physics, material properties, and system-level implications.

---

## Overview

- Analyzed MRAM device physics including MTJ structure, spin transport, and switching mechanisms  
- Studied key trade-offs such as thermal stability, switching current, scaling limits, and process integration  
- Extended understanding from device-level physics to system-level concepts including memory hierarchy and in-memory computing  

---

## Contents

### Reports
- Material-centric analysis of MRAM  
- Fundamentals of MRAM and memory computing  
- Thermal voltage and its connection to device behavior and MRAM stability  

### Presentation
- Structural limitations of MRAM and future directions

## Motivation

Through coursework and discussions, I developed an interest in spintronics and MRAM as emerging semiconductor memory technologies.

To deepen my understanding, I independently studied the physical principles, structural challenges, and scalability issues of MRAM. This process helped me connect fundamental device physics with broader system-level applications.
